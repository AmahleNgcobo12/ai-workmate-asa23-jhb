AI Workplace Productivity Assistant
A modern, responsive web application that helps professionals automate everyday workplace tasks with AI. Built as a clean, professional SaaS-style workspace with account sign-in, private saved history, and editable AI outputs.

Live app: https://ai-workmate-62.lovable.app(opens in a new tab)

Features
The Five AI Tools
Tool	What it does
Smart Email Generator	Drafts clear, professional, ready-to-edit emails with subject line, greeting, body, and closing — tuned to your chosen tone and length.
Meeting Notes Summarizer	Turns long meeting notes into a structured summary with overview, key decisions, action items, and open questions.
AI Task Planner	Converts a goal into a practical plan with priorities, ordered steps, estimated effort, dependencies, and milestones.
AI Research Assistant	Produces a knowledge-based research brief with executive summary, key findings, trade-offs, and recommended next steps.
AI Chat Assistant	A conversational thinking partner with separate, threaded conversations saved to your account.
Platform Highlights
Modern dashboard UI — clean, professional SaaS design with a sidebar navigation that works on desktop and mobile.
Structured AI prompts — every tool uses purpose-built prompts so outputs follow a consistent, professional format.
Editable AI outputs — copy, download, or edit and save any generated result.
Account & saved history — sign up with email or Google; your conversations and generated work are saved privately to your account.
Separate chat threads — each conversation gets its own page and keeps its full history across reloads.
Responsible AI — visible safeguards: outputs are clearly AI-generated, the research tool discloses it uses existing knowledge (not live web browsing), and the app never invents facts, sources, dates, or commitments. Users are reminded to review outputs and avoid sharing sensitive information.

Getting Started
Run locally
git clone <this-repository-url>
cd <repository-name>
npm i
npm run dev
Then open the local URL shown in your terminal.

Use the hosted app
Open the live app link above.
Sign in with email or Google.
Pick a tool from the sidebar, describe your task, and generate.
Edit, copy, or download the result — it's saved to your account automatically.
Privacy & Responsible Use
All conversations and generated outputs are private to your account.
The AI can make mistakes — always review outputs before using them.
Avoid pasting confidential or sensitive information into prompts.
